FAMILY 01 — THE ENGINE

Workflows that create algorithms.

These are the systems that literally produce new algorithms. Every one runs the same loop: propose variants of a procedure → evaluate against a signal the procedure can't game → keep only what measurably wins → repeat on unseen instances. That loop is GOLD at machine speed.

The synthesis — the algorithm that makes gold

Family 1 is the engine. Propose → evaluate against an ungameable signal → keep winners → repeat. AlphaEvolve, FunSearch, and the Darwin Gödel Machine are GOLD at machine speed — but none of them ship the verification discipline as a standalone standard. That gap is real, and it's the positioning.

Family 2 is the receipt. freeze before you look → same fight for both sides → score against a sealed truth → demand the paired win survives bootstrap and correction → prove it isn't the prior, the proxy, or the compute → repeat on seeds never seen.

Family 3 is the dealer. Hook with the gap, anchor on the real number, keep the loop open — and let the honesty be the hook. Family 1 is mostly DeepMind/Sakana work; GOLD is the evaluation-and-verification discipline every one of those loops needs but none of them ship.
